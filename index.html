03 / THE REVEAL

Why is it a tie?

The two cards are eliminated in different ways, but there's a surprisingly simple argument that works for either one.

51
Take out the target card.

Remove the A♠ or the 2♣, then shuffle the other 51 cards. Find their first ace.

52
Choose an insertion gap.

There are 52 equally likely places to put the removed card back: before, between, or after the 51 cards.

1
Only one gap wins.

Exactly one gap puts that card immediately after the first ace, without changing which ace comes first.

THE MATHEMATICAL RESULT
A♠ = 2♣ = 1 / 52

Each card has exactly a 1.923% chance of appearing immediately after the first ace. Different stories, identical odds.
